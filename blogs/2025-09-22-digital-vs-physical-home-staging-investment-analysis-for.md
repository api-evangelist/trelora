Choosing the right staging approach can significantly impact your home’s marketability and final sale price. Today’s sellers have two primary staging strategies: digital staging and physical staging. Digital staging uses computer-generated furnishings to enhance listing photos, while physical staging involves renting actual furniture for buyer showings.
